# Original procedural sound effects

The sound synthesis code and sound designs in `src2d/audio.js` were authored for Órbita: Frontera. No third-party recordings, music, voices or sound samples are included.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these sound designs and their generated audio output to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Cues: interface click, confirmation, error, mining, suit-transmitted steps, jump, landing, crafting, doors, suit equipment, alarm, medal, countdown, capsule separation, Earth transport engine and rocket engine.

The generated effects are artistic cues, not NASA recordings. On the Moon exterior, movement sounds represent vibration transmitted through the suit. Engine ambience is used during the Earth launch cinematic; no external sound propagating through lunar vacuum is simulated.
